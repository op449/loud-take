Most app content goes nowhere.

Building a great consumer app is hard enough. Managing short-form video internally burns cash, energy, and momentum.

01

Freelancers flake.

College creators disappear mid-campaign, ignore briefs, or miss deadlines. You spend 20 hours a week chasing raw assets on Slack instead of building your product.

02

THE OPERATING MODEL

How it works.

A three-stage flywheel designed for speed, cultural signal, and scalable volume.

STEP 01

Real creators test hooks.

We deploy native US creators to film and post dozens of distinct angles, problem statements, and visual formats. We find the real emotional trigger that makes users stop scrolling.

STEP 02

Data picks the winners.

We track 3-second hook retention, completion rates, and comment engagement in real time. Creative with outlier performance is flagged immediately for scale.

STEP 03

AI avatars multiply them.

We take winning concepts and branch them across photorealistic AI avatars and demographic variations—generating high-volume distribution without linear creator costs.
